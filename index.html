FOUNDER-LED ENGINEERING

Invented in the UK.

OMNX is a UK-based, founder-led original recovery technology brand built through hands-on work across product design, electronics, mechanics, materials, firmware, and real-world user testing. It began with a simple question: what if recovery could feel more personal, tactile, and less like cold fitness equipment?

THE FOUNDER STORY

From a simple question to working MVP devices.

OMGO began with a simple observation: many recovery tools are powerful, but they often feel cold, clinical, and easy to forget. A massage device tends to wait in a drawer until soreness appears; a tactile object, by contrast, invites the hand before the mind has even decided to use it.

The early idea was inspired by that difference. What if recovery could feel less like a task and more like something you naturally want to pick up, hold, twist, personalise, and return to? That question led OMNX toward a new kind of recovery object: one shaped around movement, touch, vibration, and daily interaction rather than utility alone.

Turning that idea into a credible product required a full clean-slate engineering effort. OMNX has been built through hands-on work across product design, electronics, mechanics, materials, firmware, prototyping, supplier development, and real-world testing — with every surface, internal structure, vibration feel, and interaction refined toward a more human product experience.

The result is not just another massage device, but the beginning of a founder-led recovery technology brand: playful on the surface, serious underneath, and designed to make personal recovery feel more expressive, more tactile, and easier to build into everyday life.

THE WHY

Recovery tools are often powerful, but they can feel intense, functional, and impersonal. OMNX is building a personalised recovery technology that feels more human, expressive, and easier to return to every day.

Many devices focus on speed, strength, and utility alone. OMNX starts from a different belief: recovery should not feel like another cold fitness task. It should feel personal, tactile, and naturally part of daily movement.
