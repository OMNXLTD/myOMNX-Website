FOUNDER-LED ENGINEERING

Invented in the UK.

OMNX is a UK-based, founder-led brand developing original recovery technology through hands-on work across product design, electronics, mechanics, materials, firmware, and real-world user testing. It began with a simple question: what if recovery technology could give people more choice, control and a clearer way to find what feels suitable for the moment?

THE FOUNDER STORY

From a simple question to working devices.

OMGO began with a simple observation: many recovery tools are powerful, but they often feel cold, clinical, and easy to forget. Many recovery devices are used only when soreness appears; a tactile object, by contrast, invites the hand before the mind has even decided to use it.

The early idea was inspired by that difference. What if recovery could feel less like a task and more like something designed to be picked up, held, twisted, personalised and returned to? That question led OMNX toward a new kind of recovery object: one shaped around movement, touch, vibration, and daily interaction rather than utility alone.

Turning that idea into a credible product required a full clean-slate engineering effort. OMNX has been built through hands-on work across product design, electronics, mechanics, materials, firmware, prototyping, supplier development, and real-world testing — with every surface, internal structure, vibration feel, and interaction refined toward a more human product experience.

The result is OMGO, the first product from a founder-led recovery technology brand. It is playful on the surface, serious underneath, and designed to make personal recovery feel more expressive, tactile, and easier to build into everyday life.

THE WHY

Recovery technology should be designed as an experience, not simply built as equipment.

A massager's speed, force and utility matter, but they are only part of the whole user experience. Touch, sound, rhythm, comfort, context and user choice all shape how a recovery routine feels. OMNX brings these elements together in a more personal, controllable approach to everyday recovery.

THE NEAR FUTURE · PLANNED FOR 2027

myOMNX+

The long-term vision is an AI-driven recovery platform that connects physical devices via Bluetooth with smarter guidance, personal routines, and human-centred recovery data.

THE GUIDANCE AND LEARNING LAYER

myOMNX+ is the future software layer for OMGO.

myOMNX+ is intended to connect OMGO devices with simple, context-led guidance. The user chooses what they want from the session and provides relevant context; the platform offers a conservative starting routine, explains it clearly, and keeps adjustment and stopping in the user’s hands.

Over time, stated preferences and previous self-reported responses may help refine future starting points. This is response-led personalisation—not a diagnosis, a universal recovery score, or a claim that the system knows what the body needs.

Development will happen in stages: first supporting safe device use and guided routines, then testing whether personalisation improves suitability, user understanding and voluntary repeat use. Success will be measured by whether people can understand, complete and return to suitable routines—not by how long they spend in the app.

Clear guidance

Short, goal-led routines with straightforward instructions, conservative starting points and clear user control.

Response-led personalisation

User choices, preferences and previously reported responses to refine future suggestions rather than prescribing an assumed optimum.

Learning through use

Help users notice what felt suitable, connect useful routines to real-life moments and need less prompting as confidence grows.

AI may support routine selection and learning in the future, but it remains an enabling capability—not the promise, authority or personality of myOMNX+.

Data principle: collect only what is needed for the chosen experience, explain why it is used, and keep optional permissions separate.
